# Banking for Purdue students

Two commonly recommended options for Purdue international students:

- **Purdue Federal Credit Union (PFCU)** — a financial cooperative serving the
  Purdue community. Notably, **students can apply for accounts without a
  Social Security Number**, making it the more accessible option for
  newly-arrived students who haven't started the SSN process yet.
- **Chase Bank** — a large national bank with locations across the country.
  Tends to be preferred by students who already have on-campus employment or
  fellowship/assistantship funding (i.e. often opened a bit later, after
  income is flowing).

Both are described by Purdue's own resource guide as offering "very similar
benefits" — the practical difference is PFCU's no-SSN-required application,
which matters most for a student who hasn't secured a job yet.
