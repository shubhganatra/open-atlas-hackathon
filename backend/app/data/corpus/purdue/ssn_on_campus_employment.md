# SSN eligibility for on-campus employment (Purdue)

Purdue's International Students and Scholars (ISS) office uses a DocuSign-based
process, notably different in mechanics from Texas A&M's (useful for the
copilot to explain school-specific quirks rather than a generic answer):

1. Once a student has an on-campus job (F-1 or J-1), they contact their
   **employing department's business office or payroll center** and request a
   "Social Security Request DocuSign form."
2. The department's business office completes their part of the form; it is
   then **routed directly to ISS** for review and signature.
3. ISS reviews the student's F-1/J-1 compliance record, then signs.
4. ISS emails the student when the **signed original** is ready for pick-up.
   The SSA office requires the **original document with a wet (physical) ink
   signature** from ISS — a PDF/digital copy is not accepted at the SSA
   office.
5. The student schedules their own appointment with the Social Security
   Administration office.

Documents needed at the SSA appointment: I-20 (or DS-2019 for J-1), passport,
visa, I-94, and proof of employment (the offer letter, or the SSN Request Form
itself from the employing department).

For on-campus contractor jobs (e.g. dining services vendors), students request
SSN form access via MyISS > General Services > Ask Student Counselor instead
of going through a department business office.
