# Banking for Texas A&M students

- International students need a **US bank account** to receive refunds from
  Student Business Services — an international/foreign bank account cannot be
  used for that purpose.
- Texas A&M's partner banking option is **Aggieland Credit Union**, offering
  an "Aggie Student Checking" account with no monthly fees and no minimum
  balance requirement, plus a mobile banking app.
- No SSN is required to open a checking account — passport + I-20 is
  generally sufficient (confirm current requirements directly with the bank,
  as policies vary).
- For **paying tuition from abroad**, A&M recommends **Flywire**, which lets
  students pay securely from any country/bank, often in their home currency.
