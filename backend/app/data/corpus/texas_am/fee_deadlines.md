# Fall 2026 tuition & fee payment deadlines (Texas A&M)

- **Full payment due in full by 5 p.m. on August 21, 2026.**
- Installment plan (if enrolled instead of paying in full):
  - 1st installment due by 5 p.m. on August 21, 2026
  - 2nd installment due by 5 p.m. on September 18, 2026
  - 3rd installment due by 5 p.m. on October 16, 2026
  - 4th installment due by 5 p.m. on November 20, 2026
- Students may enroll in the installment plan through **September 18, 2026**.

Missing the August 21 deadline without an installment plan in place risks a
hold on registration — confirm current-year specifics with Student Business
Services (sbs@tamu.edu / (979) 847-3337) since dates shift year to year.
