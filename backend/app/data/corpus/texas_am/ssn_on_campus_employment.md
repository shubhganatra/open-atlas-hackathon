# SSN eligibility for on-campus employment (Texas A&M)

Texas A&M's International Student Services (ISSS, part of Global Engagement /
International Affairs) issues the letter that certifies an F-1 student is
eligible for on-campus employment and therefore eligible to apply for a Social
Security Number (SSN).

Key requirements:

- Students must have their **ISSS Check-In complete** before they can apply
  for an SSN — this happens automatically as part of new-student arrival
  processing at A&M, but confirm it's done before starting the SSN process.
- Students may **begin on-campus work before receiving an SSN** — the SSN
  itself is not a prerequisite to start the job, only to get paid via normal
  payroll long-term. Employers can process new hires with a "SSN applied for"
  status temporarily.
- Once ISSS issues the SSN eligibility letter, the student takes it — along
  with I-20, passport, and I-94 — to the nearest Social Security
  Administration (SSA) office to apply in person.

Sequence: on-campus job offer → confirm ISSS Check-In is complete → request
SSN eligibility letter from ISSS → visit SSA office with letter + I-20 +
passport + I-94.
